Notes
- All text in the logo SVGs is converted to outlines. No fonts are required to use them.
- Typefaces: Work Sans (Bold, Regular) and Geist Mono Bold, both under the SIL Open Font License.
- CMYK values are approximate conversions. Confirm with your printer. No Pantone values are specified.
- Business card and email signature text are placeholders. Replace before use.
