## Authoritative records

Scientific records, rights and transactions are designed to live in a relational database, and source documents, raw data and artifacts in encrypted object storage.

## Derived views

Graph and vector indexes are designed to be derived, rebuildable views that carry permission metadata. Losing a cache or an index must not affect evidence or approvals.

## One chain from source to observation

Records are designed to be linked in a chain: taxon, voucher or collection, preparation or extract, fraction, compound or material batch, and assay observation. Traditional-use claims, permissions, targets, computational runs, decisions and obligations link to that chain.

## Evidence labels

Evidence is designed to be labelled so that literature claims, tentative annotations, confirmed structures, computational predictions and measured observations are never mixed.
