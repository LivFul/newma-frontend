## Signed log first

The baseline is designed to be canonical manifests, hashes and an append-only event history, with each signature naming its signer and key version. It is the starting point for verifiable record history across the platform.

## Trust and verification

A permissioned ledger, scoped zero-knowledge proofs and settlement contracts are designed to anchor provenance between partners and bring trust and security to the platform. Public anchoring, if it were ever used, would carry only permitted commitments.

## What never leaves the record

Sensitive knowledge, structures, contracts and assay data are never published on a ledger. They stay in confidential off-chain records. A commitment is not a zero-knowledge proof: a proof verifies an encoded credential, while a commitment only fixes content.
