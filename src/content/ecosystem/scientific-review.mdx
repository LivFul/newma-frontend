## Scientists decide

Scientists approve or revise experimental work packages. Automated checks show whether the required evidence is present and consistent, and a scientist approves the interpretation and the transition.

## Independent mandatory conditions

Rights, material identity and biological evidence are separate, mandatory conditions. Computational consensus cannot replace biological evidence, and campaign thresholds are set before selection.

## Named gates

After computational selection, the proposed flow passes material confirmation, assay confirmation, biological confirmation and early-lead qualification. A candidate can be held, sent back for investigation or terminated at any gate.

## Decisions leave a trail

Gate decisions are designed to be recorded with the evidence they rest on, and a decision can be invalidated when that evidence changes. Where the demo shows signing, it carries the label Demo signature, not production key.
